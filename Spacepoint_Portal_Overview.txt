# Spacepoint Ambassador Portal - Stakeholder Overview

The Spacepoint Ambassador Portal is an ecosystem designed to scale global education outreach through a tiered network of Ambassadors, Teachers, and Instructors. The platform gamifies the recruitment and session management process using points and commission-based reward system.

## ── User Roles & Capabilities ──

### 1. Platform Administrators (Admin)
The control center of the ecosystem.
- User Management: Review and approve new Ambassador and Teacher applications.
- Operational Oversight: Create and assign targeted Tasks to Ambassadors.
- Financial Control: Review and fulfill Redemption Orders for points (swag) and commissions (cash).
- Lead Management: Monitor the B2B/distributor pipeline submitted by the network.
- System Configuration: Adjust global points-to-currency ratios and platform settings.

### 2. Global Ambassadors
The primary growth drivers of the platform.
- Recruitment: Invite Teachers and Instructors using unique referral codes.
- Task Completion: Undertake specific marketing or operational tasks to earn reward points.
- Lead Generation: Submit potential business leads to earn monetary commissions.
- Network Monitoring: Track the activity and success of recruited teachers in real-time.
- Rewards: Redeem accumulated points for branded swag or withdraw commission earnings.

### 3. Teachers & Instructors
The frontline educators delivering the Spacepoint mission.
- Session Management: Schedule and log student-facing educational sessions.
- Impact Tracking: Record the number of students reached and materials delivered.
- Earning: Accumulate points for every verified session conducted.
- Rewards: Access the swag catalog to redeem points for rewards.

## ── Key Platform Features ──
- Real-Time Notifications: Intelligent alerts with deep-linking for immediate operational actions.
- Performance Analytics: Dynamic dashboards showing student reach, conversion rates, and recruitment success.
- Automated Ledger: Secure tracking of every point earned and every dollar of commission generated.
- Impact Reporting: Automated generation of PDF reports summarizing personal and network contributions.
